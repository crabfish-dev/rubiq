RUBIQ FOR ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Publisher: Santosh A
Effective date: 30 September 2026
App: Rubiq for Android · com.elviric.rubiq

Your puzzle progress

Rubiq stores your saved cube, solve times, move counts, achievements, daily streaks, preferences, selected age band, earned theme unlocks and family theme access locally on your device. The game does not require an account or send this progress to a Rubiq server. Core puzzles can be played offline. Android cloud backup of this app's data is disabled.

Resetting statistics removes local solve history, streaks and achievements while preserving settings and the saved cube. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes local progress and theme unlocks. There is no cloud account from which to recover them.

Optional rewarded ads

Adults can choose to use Google AdMob rewarded ads to unlock extra visual themes. The reward is shown before the ad and stays unlocked on this installation after it is earned. Children and teens receive all three extra backgrounds free through local family access, without watching ads. Watching an ad is optional. Ads are not displayed during timed puzzles, and you can continue playing core puzzles without watching them.

Ad and consent services use an internet connection. Google may receive your IP address and approximate region, ad/app interaction events, technical performance information and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement and abuse prevention. This occurs through Google's Mobile Ads SDK; Rubiq does not operate a separate analytics service. Google documents encrypted transport for ad-SDK data. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Age choices and privacy controls

Rubiq is intended for a mixed audience, including children under 13, teenagers and adults. Core puzzles are available to every age group without ads. Before the optional advertising feature is enabled, the app asks for an age band rather than a date of birth and stores that choice locally. The dialog explains that choosing 18 or older starts Google privacy checks, including on later app opens. You can choose Not now and keep playing the core puzzles. Users identified as under 18, and users whose age is unknown, do not request ads or consent forms. We do not use the optional ad service to collect advertising identifiers from those users.

For adults using the optional feature, Google User Messaging Platform checks which privacy messages or choices apply. To operate this consent feature, it processes information used to determine the applicable jurisdiction, stored consent choices, and technical information about consent-request and form timing, outcomes and errors. For an already identified adult, this check may also occur when the app launches, even when no ad is loaded. Ad loading is a separate user action. The app presents required privacy messages before requesting an ad and provides a privacy-options control when required. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all processing needed to provide permitted ads or consent services. You can also use Android's advertising-ID settings. See Google's privacy-message guidance.

Sharing a challenge

If you choose to share a challenge, Rubiq passes a link containing the scramble seed, cube size and length, solve time, move count and a sender label to the sharing app you select. That app and the recipient then receive the shared content under their own practices. Rubiq does not access your contacts or send messages on your behalf. Opening a challenge landing page or an advertiser's destination involves that external site's privacy practices.

Retention and removal

Local data remains until you reset the relevant information, clear app storage or uninstall. Google handles ad and consent records according to its own policies; removing Rubiq's local data does not delete records Google already holds. We do not promise a specific Google retention period or that resetting an advertising identifier deletes historical records. Use Google's published privacy controls for data it manages. Contact the publisher below for questions about Rubiq's own data practices.

This website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising widgets or cookies set by Rubiq. Cloudflare may process technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
