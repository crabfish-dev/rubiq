Learning the puzzle

What are the circles?

Every sticker on the cube is a dot in the circle view. Each circle represents a slice of the cube. Turning a slice updates both views together, so you can follow the same puzzle in two ways.

How do I turn the cube?

Drag across a sticker to turn its row or column. Drag on empty space to rotate your view. In the circle view, drag along a circle to turn its slice, or tap a circle for a clockwise turn.

Which difficulty should I pick?

Start with Warm-up or Easy for a short scramble, then try Classic when you want a longer challenge. You can choose a 2×2, 3×3 or 4×4 cube and use undo or rewind while learning.

When does the clock start?

The timer starts with your first twist after the scramble. You can inspect the puzzle first. Pausing stops the clock and hides the puzzle until you resume.

What is the Daily Challenge?

A shared 3×3 scramble changes each day at 00:00 UTC. Complete daily puzzles to build your local streak. Android progress and achievements are saved on your device; there is no Game Center connection.

How do challenge links work?

After solving, share a challenge link with a friend to send the exact scramble and your result. Rubiq's challenge import accepts compatible links from the iOS and Android editions. If a link opens the website instead of the app, copy it and use the app's challenge import.

Backgrounds & privacy

Can I play without watching ads?

Yes. All core puzzles, daily challenges and learning tools are free. Adults can optionally watch rewarded ads for extra backgrounds. Ads never interrupt a timed solve. Children and teens get all three extra backgrounds free, without ads.

Why is an age range requested?

The optional background feature uses an age range to keep advertising services disabled for children, teens and players who have not made a choice. No date of birth is requested. Choosing 18 or older enables Google privacy checks, including on later app opens; loading and watching an ad are separate actions. Choose Not now to keep playing the core puzzles. Read the Android privacy policy for details.

What if a rewarded ad is unavailable?

You can continue playing. Ad availability depends on an internet connection, privacy choices and the ad service. An extra background is unlocked when the ad service confirms the reward.

Your progress

Where is my progress stored?

Your saved puzzle, solve history, achievements, settings and theme access stay on this device. The game does not require a Rubiq account and does not back up its app data to Android cloud backup. There is no cloud account from which to restore progress after clearing app storage or uninstalling.

What does resetting statistics remove?

The reset control in Settings removes local solve history, streaks and achievements. It preserves settings and the saved cube. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes all local progress and theme unlocks.
