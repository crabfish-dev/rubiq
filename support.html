How to play

What are the circles?

Every sticker on the cube is a dot where two circles cross. There are three families of circles, one per axis, and each circle is one slice of the cube. Turning a slice moves its dots around its circle, and the face on the end of that slice spins in place.

How do I turn the cube?

Drag across a sticker on the 3D cube to turn its row or column. Drag on empty space to rotate your view. In the circle graph, drag along a circle to turn that slice, or tap a circle to turn it clockwise.

When does the clock start?

After the scramble finishes, you can look as long as you like. The clock starts on your first twist and stops the moment the cube is solved. Pausing hides the cube.

Which difficulty should I pick?

Warm-up scrambles with 3 twists and Easy with 6, so you can usually solve them by undoing the twists. Classic is a full random scramble for experienced solvers.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Everyone gets the same 3×3 scramble each day. A new one arrives at 00:00 UTC. Finish it to grow your streak. Only your first solve of the day goes on the Game Center daily leaderboard.

How do challenge links work?

After a solve, tap Challenge a friend. The link carries the exact scramble and your time. When your friend opens it in Rubiq, they play the same puzzle against your time.

Game Center

Leaderboards or Friends aren't working

Make sure you're signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center, then reopen Rubiq. Leaderboard ranks can take a minute to update.

My achievements didn't sync

Achievements you earned while signed out are sent to Game Center the next time you open Rubiq while signed in.

Your data

How do I reset my stats?

Go to Settings → Reset all stats & achievements. This clears data on your device only; Game Center history is managed by Apple.

Does Rubiq collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
