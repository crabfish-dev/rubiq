One cube.

Rubiq maps a twisty cube puzzle onto nine intersecting circles, one dot per sticker. Every circle is a slice. Turn the cube or turn a circle, and the other view follows.

Download on the App Store How to play

Two views, one state

Drag a sticker on the 3D cube or a circle in the graph. Both animate together, always in sync.

Beat the clock

The timer starts on your first twist. Chase your personal best and your average of 5.

Daily Challenge

One scramble for everyone each day, with a par to beat and a streak to keep.

Challenge friends

Send a link with your exact scramble and time. They play the same puzzle and try to beat you.

Game Center

Leaderboards, 14 achievements, and friends, all through Apple's Game Center.

From 2×2 to 4×4

Warm-up, Easy and Classic scrambles, so anyone can finish their first solve.
