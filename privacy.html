Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · Applies to the Rubiq iOS app and this website.

Short version: Rubiq does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no accounts, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your solve times, move counts, daily streak, achievements and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us. You can erase them any time in Settings → Reset all stats & achievements, or by deleting the app.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Rubiq submits your scores, lifetime solve count and achievements to Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and to your Game Center friends. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID or contact details. You can use Rubiq fully without Game Center, and you can manage Game Center in iOS Settings.

Challenge links

When you tap Challenge a friend, Rubiq creates a link that contains the scramble, your time and move count, and, if you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center display name. The link is only sent if you choose to share it, and only to the people you pick. Anyone who has the link can see what it contains.

This website

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. It uses no cookies and no analytics. Like any web host, Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Children

Rubiq is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
